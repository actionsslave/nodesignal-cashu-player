5Nodesignal-Showcase — eine Seite, zwei gleichrangige Zahlungsquellen

What the specification changes about the design

Three things reshape the interface. It is one podcast on one page, so the library, the show page and the search state fall away and the four areas — Folgen, Player, Wallet, Einstellungen — become sections of a single scroll with an index in the masthead. The wallet is no longer one balance but two equal-rank sources side by side, exactly one of them active, each carrying its own balance, its own mint intersection and its own reason for being unavailable. And the NIP-60 source introduces a quantity the old design had no place for: the Float — money taken out of the nostr wallet once per session and spent locally — which means the wallet has to show three numbers at once (Wallet, Float, in dieser Sitzung gesendet) and the history has to name the source and distinguish Entnahme and Rückgabe from actual payments. Sending stays as it was; receiving never existed.

Derived, not specified: the four areas sit on one scroll with a sticky index rather than in-page tabs (SFR-05 forbids routing, not anchors); the player pins to the bottom as a slim strip once scrolled past, so paying stays visible while reading; and money-moving actions (Float-Entnahme, Quellenwechsel, Export) each get a confirmation, since SNR-06 requires explicit consent before any payment.

4Wallet — Aufladen, Export, Mints, Nutzap senden

What the wallet has to do, given nutzaps only go one way

Money enters the wallet in exactly one way — you paste in a Cashu token — and leaves in two: as a nutzap to a podcast, or exported back out as a token. There is no receiving address to publish and no inbox to clear, so the page is a balance, those two doors, and the mints the balance sits at. The mint detail stays prominent because it is the part that carries risk: a token is a claim on one specific mint, and sending needs a mint the recipient actually lists. The npub is identity only; the balance lives on this device.
